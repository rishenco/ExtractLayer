# Vision

Answer these in your own words and delete the prompts. Agents read this before non-trivial work and trust it over their own inference, so a wrong line here is worse than a missing one.

## What it is

What the product does, in the words a user would use. Then the adjacent thing people will mistake it for, and why it is not that.

## Who it is for

The first user, specific enough to rule things out. Name who it is **not** for — that sentence does most of the work.

## What has to be excellent

The one thing that, if it is mediocre, makes the rest pointless. Rank everything else below it.

## Open tensions

What you know is unresolved. Writing it here stops agents from silently resolving it for you and calling that an inference. Delete a tension when a decision closes it.
